Privacy policy

Effective 20 November 2026

This policy describes how FTOers handles account information, solve results and recordings across its websites, including the FTO simulator and algorithm finder. FTOers is maintained by Abid Ibn Ashraf. Privacy questions and account-deletion requests can be sent to abid.ibn.ashraf@gmail.com.

Account information and Google sign-in

Accounts use Supabase Auth. We store your chosen username, email address and authentication records needed to operate your account. When you choose Google sign-in, Google provides an account identifier, email address and basic profile information, which may include your name and profile picture. We use this Google information to authenticate you and maintain your FTOers account. FTOers does not request access to your Gmail messages, Google Drive files or contacts.

Optional WCA accounts

You may sign in with or connect a World Cube Association account. WCA provides your account identifier, public name, profile picture if available, and WCA ID if you have one. We request only public profile access. You can also enter a WCA ID manually; that ID is self-reported until you connect the matching WCA account. WCA IDs and their verification status appear beside usernames on the public leaderboard. Connecting a WCA account keeps your existing FTOers profile and results.

Combining accounts

If you choose to merge existing profiles, you must authenticate both accounts and confirm which profile to keep. Both sign-in methods then access that profile. Results, review history and shared recording links are combined without duplicating recordings; sharing quota usage is combined too. Previous usernames remain reserved for legacy sign-in. Old sessions must sign in again. Short-lived merge verification records contain account identifiers and a profile summary, not passwords or OAuth access tokens. Browser-local albums remain on their original devices.

Solves, recordings and public information

The simulator stores settings, solve history and recording albums on your device. Signed-in online 3 × 3 attempts can also send solve information and recordings to our backend to calculate eligible leaderboard results. A qualifying result stays private until you choose Submit to leaderboard; it is then sent for administrator review. Recordings may contain scramble and move data, timestamps, camera and pointer movements, and keyboard inputs captured within the simulator.

Approved leaderboard results publish the solver’s username, result and date. Recordings of the current top ten approved singles are publicly watchable. Unsubmitted and pending recording evidence is stored privately and may be accessed by administrators for review. Approval or rejection creates a private account notification, including any message addressed to you by the reviewer. Your account email is not included as a public leaderboard field.

Creating a temporary link uploads that recording. Anyone holding the link can watch it and can save a copy. Sharing the same solve reuses its existing link; re-sharing an active link does not extend its expiry.

Storage and retention

  • Temporary recording links expire 30 days after creation. Expired links stop working immediately; a scheduled cleanup removes expired recordings. Deliberately re-creating an expired share uses the same URL with a new 30-day lifetime.
  • Unsubmitted recording evidence expires after 30 days. Submitted evidence expires 30 days after submission unless retained as an approved current top-ten single. Limited result metadata may remain.
  • Decision notifications are limited to the latest 50 per account and expire after 90 days. Their read state is stored with your account.
  • At most 12 recent server attempt records per solver are kept. Inactive attempt records are removed after two days.
  • Approved result metadata remains until removed through administration or account deletion. Permanent public replay evidence is retained for the current top ten approved singles; other reviewed evidence is pruned.
  • Browser-local data remains on your device until you remove it. Signing out does not erase your local solve history or album.

Service providers and browser storage

Firebase Hosting serves the websites. Supabase provides account authentication and database hosting. Brevo delivers account confirmation and password-reset emails on our behalf. Google or the World Cube Association processes sign-in when you select that option. These providers may process technical information, including IP addresses and service logs, under their own policies. Authentication and simulator preferences use browser storage to keep you signed in and preserve your data.

Username sign-in uses a hash of the request IP address for short-window rate-limit counters. The current FTOers application does not include advertising trackers. We do not sell Google account information or use it for advertising.

Your choices and requests

You can use the simulator without signing in; those solves do not enter the online leaderboard. You can sign out, export supported local simulator data and remove browser-local data. You can also revoke FTOers access through your Google account settings. Revoking Google access does not itself delete an existing FTOers account.

To request access, correction or deletion of account information, contact the email above using the address associated with your account. Account deletion removes account-owned backend records. Recordings or copies that others have already downloaded or shared outside FTOers cannot be recalled by deleting your account.

Policy changes

This page will be updated when the service’s data practices change. Material changes should be reviewed before you continue using the affected account or sharing features.
