These terms cover the FTOers websites, including the simulator, algorithm finder, accounts, recording links and leaderboard. FTOers is maintained by Abid Ibn Ashraf. Questions can be sent to abid.ibn.ashraf@gmail.com.

Accounts

Use an email address or Google account that you control. Keep your sign-in credentials private and choose a username that does not impersonate another person. Accounts and shared sessions operate across FTOers websites on the same domain.

Leaderboard submissions

The online leaderboard covers 3 × 3 FTO simulator personal bests for single, ao5 and ao12. Eligible attempts must be made while signed in and connected to the internet. An undo makes that single ineligible and counts as DNF in leaderboard averages. Averages use consecutive attempts and discard the fastest and slowest result.

Results are sent for review only when you choose Submit to leaderboard. A result must improve your approved leaderboard entry, or be your first entry in that category. A newer explicit submission replaces your pending submission in that category. Every submitted result requires administrator approval. Administrators may reject or remove inaccurate, manipulated or unsupported results. Do not forge recordings, alter timing evidence, automate false submissions or evade storage and request limits. The leaderboard is a community simulator ranking.

Recordings and sharing

Share recordings only when you have permission to share their contents. A temporary link is accessible to anyone who has it. Current top-ten approved single recordings are public. Temporary links expire after 30 days, and storage limits apply. Other people may save recordings before a link expires.

Availability and local data

Features may change, become unavailable or encounter errors. Keep your own backups of important local recordings and solve history using the available export tools. Browser-local data can be lost when browser storage is cleared or a device is changed.

Privacy and moderation

The privacy policy explains account data, Google sign-in, recordings and retention. Access or submissions may be restricted when necessary to address abuse, protect accounts or maintain the service. Contact the maintainer if you believe an account or leaderboard action is mistaken.
